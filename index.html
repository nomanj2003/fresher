Stanford researchers call the evidence early and descriptive, and find the drop comes mainly from less hiring, not more layoffs. The freshers who get through will be the ones who can show the work.

Rules we will not break.

No guaranteed jobs.

Nobody honest can promise one. We promise evidence, and every outcome we publish counts everyone who enrolled.

Private by default.

Employers see your profile only when you approve it. We never sell your data and delete it on request. Built for India's DPDP Rules, which phase in through May 2027.

AI helps. Rubrics decide.

AI gives fast feedback while you practise. Verification is scored against employer-defined standards, so it means something to the people hiring.

The scorecard we will publish

After every cohort, good or bad. Each rate counts everyone who enrolled.

Questions.

Is it free?

Joining the waitlist is free, and students are never asked to pay for a placement promise. We will publish pricing before any programme opens.

Do you guarantee a job?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We help you build proof and meet teams that are hiring, and we publish what happens next.

Which roles do you start with?

Tech and tech-enabled entry-level roles, beginning with software, data and analytics. More tracks follow as employer demand grows.

How do you verify skills?

You complete role-based tasks scored against rubrics written with employers. Your profile shows what you produced and how it was rated. Certificates alone never count.

Who owns my data?

You do. We never sell it, employers see your profile only when you approve it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Write to hello@fresher.club with a line about what you need. A founder will reply.
